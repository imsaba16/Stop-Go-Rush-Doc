# Privacy Policy for Stop Go Rush

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

**Stop Go Rush** ("we", "our", or "the Game") is a casual 2.5D arcade game. We take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled.

---

## 1. Summary: Zero Personal Data Collected
- **No account creation or registration** is required.
- **No personally identifiable information (PII)** is collected, stored, tracked, or sold.
- **No sensitive device access** (No camera, microphone, GPS location, or contact list).

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
The game stores basic preferences and progress **strictly on your local device**:
- **High Scores & Stats:** Highest scores, total cars cleared, near-miss streaks.
- **Game Settings:** Music volume, SFX volume, chosen control scheme, and tutorial completion state.

*This data never leaves your device and is not sent to any proprietary server. You can reset or delete this data at any time by clearing app data in your Android settings or uninstalling the app.*

---

## 3. Permissions Used
- **`android.permission.VIBRATE`**: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback (vibrations) during collisions, near-misses, and traffic light switching.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services
- **Google Play Games Services:** If you sign in with Google Play Games, your achievements and leaderboard rankings are managed by Google LLC under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not collect or store your Google credentials.

---

## 5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR)
Stop Go Rush does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. It is safe for all age groups.
