Privacy Policy

Stop Go Rush

Last Updated: September 19, 2026
Summary: Stop Go Rush does not require account creation, does not collect personally identifiable information, does not track your location, and does not sell or share user data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Stop Go Rush ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles information. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Stop Go Rush is an arcade game designed to be enjoyed offline or casually, and does not require registration, login credentials, or personal identification.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any of the following:

  • No personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.
  • No account registration, user credentials, or passwords.
  • No location data, GPS data, or IP tracking.
  • No device contacts, photos, files, camera, or microphone access.
  • No payment or financial information (the game does not collect payment details).

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide a seamless gameplay experience, the Game stores non-personal preferences and game progress directly on your device's local storage:

  • Game Progress & Records: High scores, cars cleared, near-miss counts, and combo records.
  • Preferences: Audio settings (Music, SFX volumes), chosen control scheme, and tutorial completion flag.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any proprietary server. You can reset or delete this data at any time by clearing the application's storage in your device's Android settings or by uninstalling the application.

4. Device Permissions

The Game requests standard permissions solely for in-game mechanics:

  • Vibrate (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay events (such as vehicle collisions, near-misses, and traffic light switches).

5. Third-Party Services

The Game may integrate with official Google Play services:

  • Google Play Games Services: If you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your high score and achievement progress are managed directly by Google LLC under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not host or operate private backend servers to store or access this data. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Stop Go Rush does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your country). Because the Game collects no personal information whatsoever, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be published directly to this URL with an updated revision date.
